A bit about me

Good design starts with a good question.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer based in Bengaluru, working across product, identity and the spaces in between. I like untangling knots, finding the human story inside the brief and making work that earns its place.

Over the last 7 years, I’ve partnered with early-stage teams and thoughtful brands to make their next chapter clearer.
